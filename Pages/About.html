Professional Profile

Who I Am

My name is Standfast Tonye Bluejack. I am an Oil and Gas Field Operator with over seven years of industry experience across production operations and plant environments.

My professional experience includes production monitoring, S&W testing, process parameter adjustment, equipment isolation and de-isolation, operational troubleshooting, Process Plant Black Start activities, and preventive maintenance routines on Open and Closed Drain Systems.

Throughout my career, I have worked in operational environments associated with Seplat Energy and ExxonMobil, supporting safe and reliable production activities while coordinating with Control Room Operators, maintenance personnel and other operations team members.

I also gained electrical project experience at the NLNG Plant, Train 5, where I supported electrical installation activities, earthing systems, cable work, inspections and troubleshooting in line with project requirements and site HSE standards.

I am committed to safe work practices, continuous learning, operational discipline and effective teamwork. I take pride in identifying abnormal operating conditions, communicating issues clearly and supporting actions that contribute to stable facility operations.

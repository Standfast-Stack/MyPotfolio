About Me

My professional background and career focus

I am an Oil and Gas Field Operator with over seven years of industry experience, specialising in production operations, process monitoring, equipment reliability and operational troubleshooting.

Throughout my career, I have gained practical experience monitoring production processes, conducting S&W tests, regulating process variables, carrying out authorised equipment isolation and de-isolation, and supporting process plant start-up activities.

I have worked in operational environments associated with Seplat Energy and ExxonMobil, collaborating with field operators, control room personnel and maintenance teams.

My professional background also includes electrical project experience at NLNG Plant, Train 5, which strengthened my understanding of industrial electrical installations and plant safety requirements.

# Standfast Tonye Bluejack — Field Operator Portfolio

## Project structure

- `index.html` — homepage
- `pages/` — About, Experience, Competencies, Equipment, Projects, Training, Education, Articles and Contact
- `assets/styles/main.css` — shared design system and page styles
- `assets/scripts/main.js` — shared navigation, contact form and page interactions
- `assets/images/` — add your approved professional profile photo and other permitted images
- `assets/certificates/` — optional certificate images/PDFs for public display
- `documents/Professional-CV.pdf` — add your final CV PDF here

## Preview locally

1. Extract this ZIP file.
2. Keep the folder structure unchanged.
3. Add your CV PDF at `documents/Professional-CV.pdf`.
4. Add your photo at `assets/images/profile-photo.jpg` if you want it on the homepage.
5. Open `index.html` in a modern browser.

## Important notes

- The Contact form prepares an email in the visitor's default email application; it does not store or directly submit form data.
- Training/authorisation entries should be checked against original certificates and validity dates before public release.
- Articles are presented as technical learning topics, not as previously published papers.
- Do not upload confidential plant information, proprietary procedures, P&IDs, sensitive operating parameters or site photographs without permission.
- The original ZIP did not include the CV PDF, profile photograph or certificate files.
